Privacy policy

OpenClaw gog is a private automation client with a single user: the Google account owner who authorised it. This policy describes what happens to data it touches.

Whose data

Only the data of the authorising account holder. No other person's Google data is accessed, and the client cannot be used by anyone else.

What is accessed

Gmail messages and labels, Drive files in the owner's folders, Calendar events, and Google Docs â€” all within the owner's own account, and only to carry out tasks the owner has requested.

Where data goes

Data stays on equipment the owner controls: the client runs on the owner's own server, and output is written to the owner's own vault and Drive. Nothing is sold, shared, or disclosed to third parties. No analytics, no advertising, no profiling.

Retention

The client stores an OAuth refresh token and any local caches on the owner's server, encrypted at rest. Message and file content is not retained beyond the task that required it, except where the owner's own notes or filing keep a copy deliberately.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately ends the client's ability to read or change anything.
